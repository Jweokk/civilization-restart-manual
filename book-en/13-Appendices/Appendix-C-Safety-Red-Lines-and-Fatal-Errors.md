## III. Edition-Specific Quick Reference for Each Environment

- **Rainforest (50)**: clean water and the four defenses against malaria; no wet charcoal into the furnace; pre-flood checklist; the rotational-felling line.
- **Permafrost (51)**: doubled CO discipline; no impact loads on cast iron in winter; thaw-subsidence observation; the three seasonal-store ledgers.
- **Desert (52)**: no settling until the water-source survey is complete; fire prevention in solar-concentration zones; open chemical ponds forbidden.
- **Island (53)**: capped lens-water extraction; go to sea by the monsoon calendar; grounding electrodes field-measured; post-disaster recovery order (communications → power → production).

## IV. The Post-Accident Learning System

Every accident (near-misses included) is recorded under the Chapter 4 failure classification: course of events – direct cause – root cause – system correction. **Accident records are the highest-density knowledge asset in the whole book** — when revising any edition of this manual, the accident library takes precedence over theory.
