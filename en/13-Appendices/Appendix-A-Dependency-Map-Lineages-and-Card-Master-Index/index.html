    Appendix A · Master Dependency Map, Tool Lineages, and Process Card Index [Third-Round Revision]¶

    1. Master Dependency Map of the Whole Book (56-Chapter DAG)¶

    Foundation: Rules and Methods (1) → Resource Survey (2) → Measurement and Calendar (3) → Usage and the Process-Card Cycle (4)
    │
    ├─ Stone Tools (5)─┬─ Fire (6)─┬─ Wood, Fibers, and Rope (7)─┬─ Clay Chemistry (8)─→ Tool Kit (9)
    │            └→ Charcoal→Kiln Temp └→ Agriculture and Food (10)←┘
    │                        └→ Pottery (11)→ High-Temp Furnaces (12)→ Lime Mortar and Building (13)
    │                                      └→ Glass (14)  Textiles and Leather (15)
    ├─ Ores (16, incl. Mining Engineering)→ Copper (17)→ Iron (18)→ Steel (19)
    │            High-Temp Furnaces (12)─┼→ Machine Tools (20)→ Mechanical Power (21, incl. Pumps and Valves)→ Measurement Standards (22)
    │            Glass (14)+Chemistry (8/23)└→ Chemistry Tree (23, three acids and soda ash)
    ├─ Glass (14)+Machine Tools (20)→ Lenses (24)→ Quantitative Science (25, Engineering Estimation)→ Experimental Chemistry (26)→ Instruments (27)
    ├─ Cylinder Boring (20)+Boiler → Steam (28)→ Power Selection (29)
    │   Machinery (21)+Instruments (27)+Chemistry → Electromagnetism (30)→ Generators (31)→ Power Grid (32)
    │        Grid+Glass → Electronic Devices (33)→ Semiconductor Materials (34)→ Integrated Circuits (35 Parts A/B)→ Electronics Industry (36)
    ├─ Production Organization and System Governance: Carrying Capacity (37)→ Ledgers (38)→ Maintenance and Traceability (39)→ Alternatives and Redundancy (40)
    ├─ Knowledge Media and Transmission: Paper and Ink (41)→ Drafting and Versioning (42)→ Archives and Apprenticeship (43)
    ├─ Healthcare and Occupational Safety: First Aid (44)→ Infection and Hygiene (45)→ Medicines (46)→ Diagnosis and Epidemics (47)→ Obstetrics and Nutrition (48)→ Occupational Health (49)
    ├─ Four Environments (resources/sequence/failure/maintenance/alternatives): Rainforest (50) Permafrost (51) Desert (52) Island (53)
    └─ Supplementary Technology Lines: Transport and Civil Engineering (54) Telegraph Communications (55) Mathematical Tools (56)

    Feedback loops: machine tools↔instruments↔generators↔better machine tools; chemistry↔purity↔electronics↔chemical control; the human-maintenance loop (44–49)↔every chapter; the knowledge-media loop (3→4→41/42/43); the information lineage (3→4→10/22→32→33→35→36).
